Why Sagelight?

A small studio, a clear set of principles, and software that gets out of your way.

🔒

Local-first by default

Your data lives on your device. We don't run servers that hold your bills, meetings, or documents — we couldn't access them if we wanted to.

🇪🇺

GDPR is the floor, not the ceiling

Every app is designed around Articles 5, 25, and 32 of the GDPR. Privacy by design, privacy by default, security built into the architecture.

🚫

No ads. No accounts. No tracking.

No third-party SDKs, no analytics, no advertising IDs, no telemetry. Zero. Open the app, use it, close it. We never see what you do.

🍎

Native Apple frameworks only

Built with SwiftData, SwiftUI, Vision, CoreSpotlight, and CloudKit. No Electron, no React Native, no third-party platforms. Fast, light, and idiomatic.

💰

Free, with no catch

All Sagelight apps are currently free. No paywall, no subscription required, no premium feature dangled in front of you. If we ever charge, existing functionality stays free.

🌿

Built by one person, in Romania

An independent sole-proprietor studio based in the EU. No investors, no growth team, no engagement metrics — just software made with care for the people who use it.

Less noise. More signal.

Pick the app that fits your problem. Or download all three. Each one stays out of your way until you need it.
