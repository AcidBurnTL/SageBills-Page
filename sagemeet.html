Designed for real meetings

The kind of meetings where you can't take notes and pay attention at the same time — and notes from memory always miss the part that mattered.

🎙

High-Quality Recording

Capture audio in your meetings with native iOS recording. Files are stored locally first — nothing leaves your phone unless you ask.

📝

Whisper Transcription

When you choose to transcribe, audio is sent to OpenAI Whisper via your own API key. Accurate across accents, multilingual, and fast.

🤖

GPT-4o Summaries & Action Items

Get clean meeting notes, decisions, and action items. SageMeet structures the output so you can paste it directly into Notion, Linear, or email.

🔍

Semantic Search

Find a topic, a quote, or a decision across all your recorded meetings. Embeddings live in a local SQLite store on your device.

👥

Speaker Analytics

Understand participation patterns — speaking time, question rate, follow-through — powered by GPT-4o-mini.

📅

Calendar-Aware

SageMeet reads your calendar (read-only) for meeting context. We never modify your events.

☁️

iCloud Sync

Your recordings, transcripts, and notes can sync across your Apple devices via iCloud Drive — under Apple's GDPR commitments.

🔑

Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)

Use your own OpenAI API key, stored in Keychain. You pay OpenAI directly for usage. We don't run a backend, so we don't bill you.

🛡

Private Mode

One toggle disables all external API calls. Recording and local playback continue working — no data ever leaves the device.

How it works

Four steps from "press record" to "paste the action items into your tracker."

01

Record

Tap record in SageMeet or start it from a Calendar event. Audio captures natively, locally, on your iPhone.

02

Transcribe

After the meeting, choose to transcribe. Whisper handles the audio via your own API key — or skip this entirely with Private Mode.

03

Summarize

GPT-4o generates clean notes, decisions, and a list of action items. Edit anything you want, then save.

04

Search & Reuse

Semantic search finds the meeting where it was decided. Spotlight indexes everything for system-wide search.
