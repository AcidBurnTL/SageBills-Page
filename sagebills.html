Questions

Things people ask first.

Is SageBills free?
Yes — SageBills is free on the App Store with no subscription, no in-app purchases, and no account required.
Does SageBills work for Romanian utility providers?
Yes. SageBills is built for European households and works for any provider in any country. Romanian providers like Enel, Electrica, ENGIE, Hidroelectrica, RCS & RDS, Orange, Vodafone, and Digi are commonly tracked. You can add custom providers in any country.
Does SageBills send my bill data anywhere?
No. SageBills is a local-only app. Your bill data, OCR-extracted invoice text, predictions, and savings goals all stay on your device. There is no backend server, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs.
How does invoice OCR scanning work?
SageBills uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to extract amounts, dates, providers, and account numbers from photographed invoices. The image and extracted text never leave your device.
Is SageBills GDPR compliant?
Yes. Because all processing happens on-device with no data transmission to Sagelight Studio or any third party (other than optional Apple iCloud sync), SageBills is fully GDPR compliant by design.
Does SageBills work offline?
Yes. All core features — bill tracking, predictions, anomaly detection, OCR scanning, CSV export — work entirely offline. An internet connection is only needed to download the app.
