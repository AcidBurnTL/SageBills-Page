Built for what actually matters

The papers we panic-search-for during emergencies, deserve better than a kitchen drawer or a notes app screenshot.

🔐

AES-256-GCM Encryption

Every attachment is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it touches storage. Keys are derived from your master PIN via PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations.

👤

Biometric & PIN Lock

Unlock with Face ID or Touch ID, fall back to a master PIN. Constant-time PIN verification and progressive lockout protect against brute-force attempts.

📷

Built-in Document Scanner

Scan with your camera using the on-device Vision framework. No third-party scanner SDKs, no images sent to a cloud service.

⏰

Expiry Tracking & Reminders

Track expiry dates for IDs, insurance, contracts, and certifications. Get local notifications well in advance — never get caught at the airport again.

☁️

Encrypted iCloud Sync

Documents sync between your iPhone, iPad, and Mac via Apple CloudKit — after they're encrypted on-device. Your iCloud sees ciphertext, never content.

🗂

Smart Categories

Organize across identity, insurance, finance, medical, contracts, education, vehicles, and more. Search by title, issuer, reference number, or notes.

📤

Secure Sharing

Need to email your insurance card? Decrypt temporarily for a one-shot share, with the temp file securely wiped immediately afterward.

🚫

Zero Third Parties

No analytics. No ads. No tracking. No third-party SDKs. Built entirely with Apple frameworks. We never see your documents.

📜

Privacy-Respecting Logs

Security events are logged via OSLog — without ever recording PINs, encryption keys, or document content. Auditable, but never invasive.

Security in depth

SageDocs is what an iCloud-synced vault should look like when it's designed by someone who actually reads the GDPR — security at every layer, not just on the marketing page.

  • AES-256
    Authenticated encryption AES-256-GCM provides confidentiality and integrity. Every attachment is encrypted with a unique nonce.
  • PBKDF2
    Key derivation hardening PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations and a 256-bit per-installation salt makes brute force impractical.
  • Keychain
    Hardware-backed key storage Encryption keys live in the Secure Enclave-backed Keychain, biometric-protected and invalidated when biometrics change.
  • Lockout
    Progressive lockout Constant-time PIN comparison with escalating cooldowns after failed attempts. No timing leaks.
  • CloudKit
    End-to-end encrypted sync Encrypted before it ever reaches iCloud. Apple stores ciphertext only — even Apple cannot decrypt it.
