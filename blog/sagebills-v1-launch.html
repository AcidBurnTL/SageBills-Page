SageBills v1.0 — What’s Inside and What’s Coming Next

SageBills v1.0 is live on the App Store. After building quietly for several months, we are ready to share what we shipped and, just as importantly, where we are going next. This is the full, unspun version of our launch.

What Is in v1.0

40+ bill categories covering every utility type a European household encounters: electricity, gas, district heating, cold and hot water, building maintenance, internet, mobile, waste collection, TV, and more. Each category has a purpose-built icon set and supports custom sub-categories.

OCR invoice scanning using the on-device Vision framework. Point your camera at any utility invoice and SageBills extracts the amount, date, provider, and billing period automatically. No cloud upload. No third-party OCR service. Everything happens on your device.

Anomaly detection that flags months where a category exceeds your personal rolling baseline by more than a configurable threshold. You set the sensitivity; SageBills watches the numbers.

Budget goals per category, with progress tracking across the billing cycle. Set a target, log your invoices, and see how you are trending before the bill arrives.

iCloud sync across iPhone and iPad, with full macOS support via Mac Catalyst. Your data lives in your iCloud account — Sagelight Studio has no servers and no access to your financial data.

What Is Coming

Our near-term roadmap focuses on three areas. First, provider-specific invoice templates for the most common European utility companies — Enel, Electrica, E.ON, Engie, Innogy, Digi, Orange — to improve OCR accuracy for structured invoices. Second, year-over-year comparison views so you can see at a glance how this January compared to last January. Third, an export function for CSV and PDF, useful for tenants who need to submit expense records to landlords or employers.

Longer term, we are exploring a bill prediction engine that uses your historical data to forecast next month’s total before the invoices arrive. Stay tuned.
